About Me

Hola! I’m Abjoraj Biswas — a 2nd-year B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at Techno India University, a curious developer, and a builder who loves turning ideas into things that actually work.

I’m a builder, learner, and problem-solver who enjoys exploring the intersection of technology, creativity, and real-world impact.

My journey so far has taken me through IoT & Hardware, Python, AI & Machine Learning, DBMS, and App Development. I enjoy connecting the dots between hardware and software — whether that means building smart devices, developing applications, experimenting with AI, or creating something completely new from scratch.

For me, “Aprender” — to learn — is an everyday process. I believe the best way to understand technology isn't just by reading about it, but by building it, breaking it, fixing it, and trying again.

I’m especially excited by projects that combine AI, automation, IoT, and application development to solve problems beyond the screen. Hackathons and hands-on projects have taught me to think creatively, work under pressure, and turn simple ideas into working prototypes.

Right now, I’m focused on growing as a developer, sharpening my AI/ML and software skills, and exploring technologies that challenge the way I think.

Crear. Aprender. Innovar.
Create. Learn. Innovate.

That’s me — always curious, always building, always learning. 🚀
